Incidental durotomy (n = 1,549, 14 UK centres) (Tafazal & Sell 2005) M: 3.5% primary discectomy; 8.5% stenosis surgery; 13.2% revision discectomy • screen for postural headache (worse upright, relieved supine) or clear CSF wound leak
Post-op Cauda equina syndrome (CES) & epidural haematoma re-check: new urinary retention (Sn 0.90, Sp 0.99; PVR > 200 mL → MRI-confirmed CES in 43%), saddle numbness, or progressive bilateral motor deficit → same-day emergency surgical team contact (decompression < 48 h associated with better outcomes) L
