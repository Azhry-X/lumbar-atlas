Malignancy, Vascular, Inflammatory & InfectionSystemic & NICE NG59
Malignancy red flag (Downie 2013 BMJ) M: history of cancer 7% (3–16) in primary care; 33% (22–46) in emergency care
Abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA palpation, Fink 2000 Arch Intern Med) M: Sn 68% (60–76), Sp 75% (68–82), LR+ 2.7, LR− 0.43; Sn 82% for AAA ≥ 5.0 cm; Sn 91% if girth < 100 cm vs 53% if ≥ 100 cm
Axial spondyloarthritis (ASAS criteria, Rudwaleit 2011) M: Sn 79.5%, Sp 83.3% • chronic inflammatory back pain → rheumatology referral
Spinal infection E: fever/rigors, IV drug use, immunosuppression, recent spinal procedure, relentless non-mechanical pain → urgent medical workup
Imaging rule NICE NG59: “Do not routinely offer imaging in a non-specialist setting” (1.1.4); consider imaging in specialist settings “only if the result is likely to change management” (1.1.6) • ref: Jarvik & Deyo 2002
