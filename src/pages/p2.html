NICE on stratification NICE NG59: “Consider using risk stratification (for example, the STarT Back risk assessment tool) at first point of contact” (1.1.2)
STarT Back — scoring Calc (Hill 2008) M: 9 items; total 0–3 = low risk; psychosocial subscale (items 5–9) 4–5 = high risk; rest = medium
STarT Back — stratified care (RCT, n = 851) (Hill 2011 Lancet) H: RMDQ between-group difference 1.81 (1.06–2.57) at 4 months; 1.06 (0.25–1.86) at 12 months
STarT Back in older adults (Vigdal 2024) M: predictive validity poor; AUC 0.65–0.67
Örebro short form (ÖMPSQ-10) (Linton 2011) M: correlation with long form 0.91; cut-off 50 identified 85% (occupational) / 83% (primary care) of those who developed disability
