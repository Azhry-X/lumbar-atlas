3 · Segmental Mobility Testing & Clinical Instability BatteryRELIABILITY WARNING · PLET · PIT
Segmental mobility testing — reliability warning (Hicks 2003) L: κ −0.02 to 0.26 (not reliable); pain provocation κ 0.25–0.55 — rely on pain provocation rather than stiffness grading
Prone instability test (PIT) — reliability L: κ = 0.87 (Hicks 2003) BUT κ = 0.10 (−0.27 to 0.47) in a later study (Ravenna 2011) — reliability inconsistent
Passive lumbar extension (PLE) test (Kasai 2006; review confirming it as most accurate: Alqarni 2011) L: Sn 84.2%, Sp 90.4%, LR+ 8.84 (4.51–17.33)
Radiographic instability predictors (Fritz 2005 Eur Spine J) L: lumbar flexion ≥ 53° OR no hypomobility on segmental testing → LR+ 4.3 (1.8–10.6)
