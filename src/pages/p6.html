2 · Lumbar Traction (Cochrane) WEGNER 2013
Traction (Cochrane) (Wegner 2013) M: 32 RCTs, 2,762 participants; traction alone or with other treatment has little or no effect
Applies to both mechanical and manual traction in low back pain with or without sciatica; redirect treatment time to active exercise (p8).
3 · Therapeutic Ultrasound (Cochrane) EBADI 2020
Therapeutic ultrasound (Cochrane) (Ebadi 2020) L: vs placebo, short-term pain MD −7.12 (−17.99 to 3.75) on 0–100 VAS: little or no difference
No clinically important benefit over placebo; not recommended by NICE NG59 (1.2.9).
